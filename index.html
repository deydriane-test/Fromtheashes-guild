01 / OUR STORY

BUILT FROM
WHAT WAS LEFT.

FromTheAshes was born from the members of a guild that failed — not because the people failed, but because the foundation did.

We came together again with one idea: build the guild we wanted to be part of.

Leadership exists to serve the members. Decisions should be understandable. Problems should be addressed. Credit belongs to the people doing the work.
